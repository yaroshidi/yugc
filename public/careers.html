Careers

At YUGC, we’re building an AI video editor that creates beautiful videos from a simple voice request.

YUGC understands intent, finds the best moments, and turns raw footage into a complete story — with editing, rhythm, music, captions, and voiceover handled end-to-end.

We’re a product-first team that moves fast and stays honest: we focus on real user value, ship iteratively, and avoid unnecessary complexity.

If you want to work on agent-like workflows, mobile video production, and deep automation — you’ll feel at home here.

Annual paid leave: 20 working days per year.
Paid holidays: flexible 2 days per quarter (8 per year).
Paid sick leave: 7 days per year.
Learning & development: the company co-funds 50% of job-relevant courses.
